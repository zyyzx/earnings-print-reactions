How liquid US energy names trade around earnings

Nine large-cap energy names, every quarterly print since 1Q16 (373 prints): how the stock moved vs its peers on the print day and the days either side, and whether beating the number mattered. Moves in basis points vs peers (100 bps = 1%).

What the numbers say

  • The print day carries the move: 244 bps avg vs peers on the day, vs 91 the day before and 134 the day after. Day-after drift nets to +1 bps — the reaction is done by the close.
  • Beating the number is not beating peers: SLB beat consensus 93% of the time but was up vs peers on only 40% of print days; BKR is the only name above 60% (62%).
  • Where the juice is: BKR 343 bps avg print-day move vs peers, OXY 307, EOG 274; XOM the quietest at 131.
  • A rally into the print is not a signal: follow-through on the day ranges from 20% (OXY, n=5) to 71% (EOG, n=7).

Method & data

Method. "vs peers" = stock minus equal-weight peer basket ex-self (OFS: HAL/SLB/BKR · Producers: XOM/CVX/COP/EOG/OXY/FANG), close-to-close, in basis points. Print day = first full session reflecting the release (before-open → same day; after-close → next session). Consensus = mean EPS estimate the day before the print. Confidence bands are Wilson 90%. Source: S&P Capital IQ (prices, dates, estimates); release timing from company timestamps.
