How liquid US energy names trade around earnings

Every quarterly print since 1Q16 for nine large, liquid energy names: how the stock moved versus its peers the day before the print, on the print day and the day after — and how reported EPS compared with the consensus that stood the day before. All moves are in basis points (100 bps = 1%).

Method. Relative = stock minus equal-weight peer basket ex-self (OFS: HAL/SLB/BKR · Producers: XOM/CVX/COP/EOG/OXY/FANG), close-to-close, in basis points. t0 = first full session reflecting the release (BMO → announce day; AMC → next session). Consensus = mean EPS estimate the day before the print. Source: S&P Capital IQ.

What the numbers say

  • Sample: 9 liquid energy names, 373 prints since 1Q16. Average |move| vs peers is 91 bps the day before, 244 bps on the print, 134 bps the day after — the print day carries ~2.7x the day-before dispersion.
  • Most consistent underperformer on the print: SLB — outperformed peers on t0 in 40% of 42 prints (90% band 29%-53%) despite beating pre-print consensus 93% of the time. Most consistent outperformer: BKR (62%).
  • Largest print-day dispersion vs peers: BKR (avg |t0 rel| 343 bps); smallest: XOM (131 bps). Absolute print-day moves average 287 bps across the group.
  • Follow-through after a >100 bps relative rally into the print (t-1): EOG kept going on t0 in 71% of 7 cases; OXY in only 20% of 5 — pre-print strength is not a reliable signal across the group (n is small everywhere).
  • Day-after drift vs peers is small on average (+1 bps; range -51 to +40); the reaction is largely done by the close of t0.
