"vs peers" = HAL's close-to-close move minus the equal-weight average of SLB, BKR that day, in basis points (100 bps = 1%). Hover any point for the quarter.

  • The number is managed: HAL has beaten consensus in 36 of 42 prints since 1Q16. The six misses were the only prints where the stock really broke (−355 bps vs SLB/BKR on average; down vs peers on 5 of 6).
  • A beat buys almost nothing: on beat days HAL averaged +20 bps vs peers and finished ahead of them less than half the time (47% of 36). Across all 42 prints it lagged peers on 57% of print days (43% up, 90% band 31–55%).
  • Positioning shows up the day before, not the day of: HAL drifts up into the print (+30 bps vs peers on average), but a >100 bps rally into the print has been a coin flip on the day (6 of 12 up, avg −41 bps).
  • Size: 266 bps average print-day move vs peers (318 bps outright); a third of prints move more than 300 bps vs peers, from −861 bps (2Q18) to +1,094 bps (3Q25). Day-after drift is small (+20 bps).
